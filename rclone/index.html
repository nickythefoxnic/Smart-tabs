NicOS rclone Reader

A personal utility for the account owner to connect rclone to Google Drive in read-only mode.

What it does

The user authorizes the connection to list and read their own Google Drive files and metadata. The local workflow can verify backup integrity and compare preserved files with local originals. It does not request permission to modify or delete files in Google Drive.

Who uses it

This OAuth client is maintained for the owner's personal workflows. It is not a public file-hosting service, a social network, or an app offered for others to upload private data.

Where data goes

Authorized reads are initiated by rclone on the user's own computer. This informational website is static; it has no sign-in form and does not receive Google Drive content or OAuth tokens.
