Smart Tabs Privacy Policy

Effective date: November 8, 2026

Smart Tabs maps Chrome tab groups to Claude and ChatGPT project pages.

Data handled

  • Chrome tab and tab-group metadata needed for the feature.
  • Project URLs, project IDs, names, and binding settings entered or shown during user-invoked binding/setup.

Binding settings are stored in Chrome storage sync. Smart Tabs has no developer-controlled server.

Not collected

  • No analytics or advertising trackers.
  • No conversation-content collection by the developer.
  • No passwords, authentication cookies, access tokens, or payment data.
  • No sale or unrelated sharing of user data.

Permissions

tabs, tabGroups, storage, contextMenus, and scripting are used only for project-tab organization and user-invoked setup. Host access is limited to claude.ai and chatgpt.com.

Affiliation

Smart Tabs is independent and is not affiliated with Anthropic or OpenAI.
